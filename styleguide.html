01 — Colour

A restrained, clinical palette

A deep teal-blue carries authority without the generic corporate blue. Warm neutrals keep the page reading as paper. One warm clay accent is held in reserve for emphasis — used sparingly, it stays loud.

02 — Typography

Source Serif 4 for authority, Inter for clarity

A serif headline signals research and publication; a neutral grotesk keeps dense body copy effortless. Every size is fluid — it interpolates between a 360px phone and a 1280px desktop, so nothing jumps at a breakpoint.

Measure

Long-form paragraphs are capped at 64 characters. That is the width at which the eye reliably finds the start of the next line, and it is the single biggest readability lever on a text-heavy page. This paragraph sits at exactly that limit, so you can judge it directly rather than taking it on faith.
